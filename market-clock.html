MARKET X-RAY · WHY DID IT MOVE?

Reconstruct the sequence before you tell a story.

MarketIQ's Market Clock lines up public information with sportsbook price changes, measures reaction lag and propagation, and explicitly flags when movement began before the information was detected.

Methodology rule: timing is evidence of sequence, not proof of causality. The system can say “movement followed this information” without pretending the information caused the move.

PREEXISTING MOVEMENT TEST

The feature that protects us from fake explanations.

1 · LOOK BACKCheck the configured pre-information window for movement already underway.
2 · LOOK AHEADMeasure subsequent observed moves and the books participating.
3 · CLASSIFYNO_REACTION, POST_INFO, PREEXISTING, or PREEXISTING_AND_POST_INFO.
4 · NEVER REWRITEKeep the timeline even when it weakens the original theory.
